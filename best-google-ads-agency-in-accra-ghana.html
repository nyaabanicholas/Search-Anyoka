How to Choose the Right Google Ads Agency

Choosing the wrong agency can be an expensive mistake.

If you’re evaluating options, here are a few things to look for.

First, check for proven results. A good agency should be able to show case studies, real data, or examples of past success.

Second, look for local experience. An agency that understands the Ghanaian market will always have an edge.

Third, make sure they use proper conversion tracking. Without it, there’s no way to measure performance or improve campaigns.

Fourth, transparency matters. You should always know how your budget is being spent and what results you’re getting.

Finally, consider communication. A reliable agency will keep you informed, explain strategies clearly, and respond quickly when needed.

And here’s a quick tip: if an agency promises “cheap clicks,” be cautious. Cheap traffic doesn’t always mean profitable results.
